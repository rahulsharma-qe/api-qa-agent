# BOOK-101: Passengers can cancel a booking and get their money back

As a passenger, I want to cancel my booking from the app, so that I am not charged for a trip I
won't take.

- A passenger can cancel a booking with its PNR.
- Cancel within 24 hours of booking and get all your money back.
- After 24 hours, the booking can still be cancelled, but no refund is given.
- A booking that is already cancelled can't be cancelled again.
- The passenger can check the refund status of a cancelled booking in the app.
- Refunds are paid by the payments service.
